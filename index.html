Welcome, writers

A bridge between your story and its next opportunity

I'm AE Joyce, an acquisition editor working with online fiction. My goal is to help authors navigate the early steps of the publishing process with clearer expectations and practical guidance.

✍

For new authors

Learn what to prepare before submitting: an engaging opening, a clear premise, a synopsis, and a consistent writing plan.

◎

For active writers

Explore opportunities that may fit your genre, writing goals, and availability. Requirements differ between platforms and projects.
